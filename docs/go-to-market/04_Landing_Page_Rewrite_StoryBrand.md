# BidBox Landing Page Rewrite Plan (StoryBrand)

All copy below is final-draft external copy. No em dashes anywhere. Keep the current site's visual vibe (clean, blue, product screenshot right of hero); this rewrite changes what the page says, not how it looks.

---

## Part 1: The BrandScript (the source of truth for every word)

**1. A Character (the hero, not BidBox):** The owner or estimator at a small public works GC. Wants to win more contracts with the team he has. He is competent and proud; the tools are the problem, not him.

**2. With a Problem:**
- *Villain:* **The Scatter.** Dozens of agency portals, each with its own logins, formats, and buried addenda.
- *External:* Finding jobs, digging through specs, tracking deadlines and addenda, chasing sub quotes, chaotic bid days.
- *Internal:* "I'm always one missed detail away from losing a job I should have won. The big GCs have a whole department for this. I have me."
- *Philosophical:* The best builder should win the job, not the biggest back office.

**3. Meets a Guide:**
- *Empathy:* "We spent years bidding public works. We know what a 4am bid day feels like."
- *Authority:* Built by a public works operator; live coverage of CA agencies; founding GCs on board (add proof as Phase 1 produces it).

**4. Who Gives Them a Plan (three steps, everywhere, always the same):**
1. **Set your coverage.** Tell BidBox your counties and the work you do.
2. **BidBox does the digging.** Every matching bid found, documents pulled, requirements flagged, deadlines and addenda tracked.
3. **Show up ready on bid day.** Organized pursuits, coordinated subs, one-click Excel export.

**5. And Calls Them to Action:**
- *Direct CTA:* **Start Free Trial** (top right, colored button, and repeated down the page)
- *Transitional CTA:* **Get the free guide** (lead generator PDF)

**6. That Results in Success:** More bids submitted, more jobs won, same team. Calm bid days. The reputation of a $50M GC on a 3-person operation.

**7. Or Failure (stakes, used once, lightly):** The job you never saw. The addendum that disqualified you. Another year of the same revenue.

**The One-Liner (explanatory paragraph opener, email signatures, LinkedIn bios, verbatim):**
> "Public works contractors waste hours digging through agency portals and still miss bids they should have won. BidBox finds every project worth bidding, flags what it takes to qualify, and gets you ready for bid day. So you win more work without hiring another estimator."

## Part 3: Nurture sequence (6 emails after the PDF download)

1. **Day 0:** Deliver the guide + one-line story of a bid lost to a missed addendum
2. **Day 2:** Problem email. The Scatter, named and agitated. No pitch.
3. **Day 4:** Value email. "The 30-minute Monday routine that replaces portal-checking" (BidBox appears as the tool inside the routine)
4. **Day 7:** Proof email. Founding customer story with numbers
5. **Day 10:** Objection email. "Is AI extraction actually reliable?" (source-linking answer)
6. **Day 13:** Direct ask. Trial CTA + calendar link, light stakes close ("the next bid cycle starts whether you're organized or not")

Per the framework: the site is touch zero, the PDF is touches one and two, this sequence carries the brand to the eight touches it takes before the phone rings.

## Part 4: What changes vs. the current page (summary)

| Current | Rewrite | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "#1 Estimating Bid Desk" headline | "Never miss a public works bid you should have won" | Current headline names a category nobody searches; new one names the failure the buyer fears (grunt test, question 2) |
| Sells file sharing + quote collection | Sells find → qualify → prepare → win | Page catches up to the product's actual direction |
| Fake GC logo bar + placeholder testimonials | Agency coverage strip now, real proof after Phase 1 | This audience is small and skeptical; fabricated social proof is a trust landmine |
| $199 lifetime on public pricing | Tracker/Command tiers | Lifetime deal caps revenue and signals side project |
| Two competing CTAs, generic | One direct CTA (trial) + one transitional (guide) | StoryBrand CTA discipline; everything else to the junk drawer |
| No lead capture beyond newsletter | PDF lead generator + 6-email sequence | Builds the asset that converts programmatic-SEO traffic in Phase 2 |
