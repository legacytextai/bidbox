# Phase 1 Execution Sheet: The Founding Ten
**12 weeks | Goal: 10 paying customers, ≥6 weekly-active, ~$4k MRR**
**Time budget: ~20 focused hours/week (Sun–Tue founder days + evenings)**

---

## Operating rules for the whole phase

1. **Nothing new gets built for prospects who haven't paid.** Feature requests go in a log; the log gets prioritized by paying-customer overlap only.
2. **Every call gets logged same-day** in a single tracker (company, contact, source bid, stage, next step, objection heard, exact words they used to describe the pain). The exact-words column becomes the landing page copy.
3. **Phone first, email second, LinkedIn last.** This industry answers phones between 6:30–9:00am and 3:30–5:00pm.
4. **Weekly scorecard every Sunday night:** dials / conversations / demos / trials / paid / MRR. No narrative, just numbers.
5. **The offer is the Founding Ten offer (see call script doc) and it is genuinely limited to ten.** When it's gone it's gone; scarcity only works if it's real.

---

## Weeks 1–2: Ammunition

**Goal: a 150-company target list and a demo that closes.**

- [ ] Pull plan holder lists from 25–30 SoCal bids closed in the last 60 days (OC, LA, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego). Prioritize project types you know from Future Contractors: site work, concrete, general engineering, building improvements, $500k–$10M range.
- [ ] Build the tracker: company, principal/estimator name, phone, email, the specific bid(s) they pulled plans on, whether they bid, whether they won. Target: 150 qualified companies. (This research doubles as product QA on the crawler.)
- [ ] Rank the list: Tier A (bid 3+ jobs in 60 days = high-cadence, feel the pain weekly) ~40 companies; Tier B (1–2 bids) ~70; Tier C (pulled plans, didn't bid) ~40.
- [ ] Warm layer: list 15–20 people from the Future Contractors years, IRA network, subs and GCs you know personally. These are week-3 first calls.
- [ ] Build the 10-minute demo around ONE prospect's real world: load 3 live projects from agencies they actually bid. The demo script is: their county, their agency, their project types. Never demo generic data.
- [ ] Set up: Stripe checkout link for the Founding Ten offer, a Calendly (or equivalent) 15-min slot, a one-page offer summary PDF (terms from the call script doc).
- [ ] Memorize the one-liner. Index card. Verbatim, every conversation, all twelve weeks.

**Scorecard target:** 150 companies listed, 40 Tier A ranked, demo rehearsed 3x on friendlies.

## Week 3: Warm launch

**Goal: first 3 demos booked from people who already trust you.**

- [ ] Call all 15–20 warm contacts. Ask for 15 minutes OR a referral to the person at their company (or a peer company) who runs bids. Referral ask verbatim: "Who do you know that's chasing public works right now and drowning in it?"
- [ ] Run first 3–5 demos. These are practice reps AND real prospects. Record objections word-for-word.
- [ ] Close at least 1 founding customer from warm network. Concierge onboard within 48 hours: you personally load their next 3 pursuits, set their alerts, walk them through it on a screen share.
- [ ] After each demo, same-day recap email: what you set up, what happens next, the Stripe link.

**Scorecard target:** 20 conversations, 4 demos, 1 paid.

## Weeks 4–6: Cold engine on

**Goal: prove the plan-holder-list motion converts with strangers. 3–4 more paid (running total 4–5).**

Weekly rhythm (repeat all three weeks):
- [ ] 40 dials/week off Tier A then Tier B, mornings. Script in call script doc. The hook that earns 30 seconds: name the actual bid they pulled plans on.
- [ ] Same-day voicemail-plus-email for every non-answer (templates in call script doc). Second dial 3 business days later. Three touches max, then recycle to a 30-day shelf.
- [ ] 3–6 demos/week held. Every demo pre-loaded with THEIR agencies and live projects.
- [ ] Concierge onboarding within 48h of every close. First-week check-in call with every new customer: "What did you look at? What was missing?"
- [ ] Friday: 1 hour of product triage from the feature log. Fix only what blocks daily use for paying customers (deadline accuracy, addenda detection, alert reliability). Accuracy bugs are churn; features are not.

**Scorecard targets per week:** 40 dials → 12–15 conversations → 4 demos → 1–2 trials → ~1 paid.

## Week 7: Checkpoint (do not skip)

- [ ] Hard numbers review: paid count, weekly-active count, conversion at each funnel stage, top 3 objections, top 3 product gaps.
- [ ] Call every paying customer (should be ~5). One question that matters: "If BidBox disappeared Friday, what would you do Monday?" Answers containing a workflow ("I'd have to go back to checking portals every morning") = product-market signal. Answers that shrug = trouble; find out what job it's actually failing to do.
- [ ] Decision gate:
  - **On track (4–6 paid, most active weekly):** proceed, raise dial volume.
  - **Paying but not using:** freeze outreach 1 week, fix the top usage blocker, re-onboard everyone personally.
  - **Can't close at all (≤2 paid despite 100+ dials):** the pitch, the price, or the ICP tier is wrong. Re-listen to call notes; test dropping to Tier A only and/or leading with a different pain (missed addenda vs. discovery vs. bid-day chaos) before touching price.
- [ ] Update call script with the real objection handlers earned in weeks 3–6.

## Weeks 8–10: Grind to ten

**Goal: running total 8–9 paid.**

- [ ] Same weekly rhythm, volume up: 50 dials/week, 5–7 demos/week.
- [ ] Activate the referral flywheel: every happy customer gets the ask at their week-2 check-in: "Who's one GC you'd want on this? I'll give them the same founding terms under your name." Founding customers referring = the strongest possible signal.
- [ ] Refresh the target list with NEW plan holder lists from bids closing now (the list self-regenerates; that's the point).
- [ ] Start the testimonial bank: after any customer's first clear win (caught an addendum, found a job, clean bid day), ask for two sentences in their words. These replace the placeholder testimonials on the site.
- [ ] Soft-start Engine B: begin drafting the weekly Bid Report internally using data already in the system. Do NOT publish yet; build 3–4 issues of muscle memory. (This is the only Phase 2 prep allowed in Phase 1.)

## Weeks 11–12: Close it out and package the proof

- [ ] Close to 10. If sitting at 8–9, offer the final Founding slots with an explicit deadline ("Founding terms close on the 30th, then it's $449").
- [ ] Produce the proof pack (one page): 10 logos, MRR, weekly-active %, 3 quotes, 2 concrete saves ("caught Addendum 3 on the Fullerton job that changed bond requirements"). This document is simultaneously: the Phase 2 landing page ammunition, the future fundraise opener, and the referral leave-behind.
- [ ] Founding customer debrief calls (all ten): what almost stopped you from buying, what would you tell another GC, what's the one thing to build next. Record. This transcript writes the Phase 2 messaging.
- [ ] Phase 2 go/no-go against the gate: **10 paying AND ≥6 weekly-active.** Pass → launch programmatic pages + publish Bid Report issue #1 in week 13. Fail → the checkpoint protocol from week 7, extended; do not start Phase 2 marketing on top of a leaky product.
