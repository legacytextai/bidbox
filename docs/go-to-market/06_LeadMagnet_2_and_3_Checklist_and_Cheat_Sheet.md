# The Public Works Bid Day Checklist

---

**THE PUBLIC WORKS BID DAY CHECKLIST**
The last 72 hours, in order. Miss nothing.

### T-72 hours
- [ ] Latest addendum count verified against the agency posting (not against memory)
- [ ] Bid form version matches the latest addendum
- [ ] All addenda acknowledgment boxes completed
- [ ] Bond: bid bond ordered, correct percentage, correct obligee name
- [ ] Sub coverage board reviewed: every scope has at least one live quote or a named plug
- [ ] Open scopes: calls made, not emails

### T-48 hours
- [ ] All quotes consolidated into one workbook, one owner
- [ ] Scope gaps between sub quotes identified (who has the bollards?)
- [ ] Unit prices and allowances entered per the current bid schedule
- [ ] Subcontractor listing form drafted (names, licenses, DIR numbers, portions of work)
- [ ] Delivery logistics confirmed: physical drop location and deadline, or e-bid portal login tested TODAY, not tomorrow

### T-24 hours
- [ ] Final sub calls for missing trades (morning, not afternoon)
- [ ] Math check: extensions, additions, and the schedule of values foot correctly
- [ ] Signatures, acknowledgments, non-collusion declaration complete
- [ ] Second person reads the bid form against the checklist (fresh eyes rule)

### Bid morning
- [ ] Late quotes triaged: better number AND listable, or ignored
- [ ] Final number set by the deadline you control (60 minutes before theirs)
- [ ] Submitted with margin: traffic, portal lag, and clerk lines are real
- [ ] Confirmation (stamp or receipt) saved to the project file

### Within 48 hours after
- [ ] Results logged: winner, amount, your spread, spread to engineer's estimate
- [ ] One sentence: why we won or lost
- [ ] Thank-you texts to subs who covered you (they remember)

# LEAD MAGNET 3
# The California Public Works Qualification Cheat Sheet

*Design direction: 3 to 4 pages, reference-card style. This is the "keep forever" asset that earns saves and shares. Positioning note: general reference, not legal advice; include a one-line disclaimer. No em dashes.*

---

## Cover
**THE CALIFORNIA PUBLIC WORKS QUALIFICATION CHEAT SHEET**
Every requirement that can kill your bid, on four pages.

## Page 1: The License Question
- Match the license classification in the notice to your active classifications before spending a single estimating hour. A-General Engineering vs. B-General Building vs. C-specialty is the first go/no-go gate.
- Joint venture bids require the JV license, not just the members' licenses. Lead time applies.
- Out-of-classification bids are non-responsive. Agencies do not waive this.

## Page 2: DIR, Prevailing Wage, and Registration
- Public works registration with the DIR is mandatory for you AND for every listed subcontractor. An unregistered listed sub can sink an otherwise winning bid.
- Verify sub DIR numbers when you build the listing form, not after award.
- Prevailing wage determinations bind your labor pricing; confirm the applicable determination date for the bid.
- Certified payroll obligations start at award; price the administrative load, don't discover it.

## Page 3: Bonds and Prequalification
- Bid bond: typically 10%, but read the notice; percentages and forms vary by agency. Wrong form = non-responsive at some agencies.
- Performance and payment bonds at 100% of contract are the standard ask; confirm your aggregate bonding capacity covers this job PLUS current backlog before bidding.
- Prequalification: many CA agencies (especially school districts) require a prequal packet approved BEFORE bid day, sometimes weeks before. This is the single most common way GCs waste a full estimate. Check for prequal the day the project enters your pipeline.

## Page 4: The Fine Print That Disqualifies
- Mandatory job walk attendance (sign-in sheets are checked)
- Experience requirements: "N similar projects in X years" clauses, read literally by reviewers
- Subcontractor listing thresholds and the penalties for listing errors
- Addenda acknowledgment: every addendum, every box
- Bid security, signatures, notarization where required
- Delivery: e-bid portal account creation takes time; physical bids are date-time stamped and seconds count

## The One-Hour Go/No-Go Screen (pullout box)
Run these six checks the day a project appears. If any fail and can't be cured in time, kill the pursuit and spend the hours on a bid you can win.
1. License classification match
2. DIR registration current (you + likely subs)
3. Bond capacity covers this + backlog
4. Prequal required? Deadline passed?
5. Mandatory walk attended or attendable?
6. Experience requirements met on paper?
