# BidBox Company Messaging

## One-Liner (Donald Miller StoryBrand Framework)

**Problem:**
GCs miss out on great projects every week because they don't have time to find, estimate, and submit enough public works bids.

**Plan:**
We created a system that uses AI agents to handle the time-consuming, repetitive tasks in the bidding process.

**Result:**
So you finally have the capacity to compete and win the projects your business deserves.
